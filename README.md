# PFOS–MI essential inputs and final results

Edition 20, 29 October 2026. These files accompany the revised exploratory
ALPL–PLAU study. They contain necessary inputs, final numerical and structural
results, and the code and parameters needed to inspect or repeat the analyses.
Intermediate estimator caches and duplicate exports are not included.

Start with S1 `02_results/Tables.xlsx` and `03_figure_data` for manuscript values.

Manifests identify every delivered file; they do not establish scientific validity.

## Scientific scope

The primary definition is at least two strictly positive PFOS-source scores:
73 PFOS targets, 37 MI overlaps and 36 measurable candidates. Historical38,
strict18 and alternative-method sensitivities remain separately labelled.
S1 retains all 492 primary, 957 strict18 and 120 historical subset-comparison
rows, complete-workflow held-out predictions and final selection frequencies.
Nonselected-subset per-sample predictions and fitted estimator caches are omitted;
they must be regenerated by running the recorded refit procedures if needed.

All-term enrichment and transcriptome-wide expression results include negative
findings. No primary KEGG term passes BH correction. Failed ALPL, neutral-ligand
and fixed-water PLAU controls remain alongside the successful dry PLAU preparation.
External evaluations are retrospective, not untouched confirmatory tests. Missing
individual covariates are not imputed from group-level demographics.

## Verification and reproduction

Install the documented software separately; these commands do not install it.
Use fresh output directories and keep S1–S5 from the same edition together.

```text
python verify_package.py
python -m zipfile -e S2_Data.zip unpacked_S2
Rscript --vanilla unpacked_S2/03_code/replay_fixed_panel.R unpacked_S2 . NEW_SCORE_CHECK
python unpacked_S2/03_code/verify_metadata_snapshots.py unpacked_S2 NEW_METADATA_CHECK.json
```

The first command checks file integrity. Fixed-model replay recomputes the
internal and three external ALPL–PLAU scores without retraining. The metadata
check parses the three supplied GEO snapshots; it is not a replay of the older
multi-source publication/supplement audit. Final covariate findings and source
references are retained in S1.

S2 documents primary-selection refitting and restoration of retained source-code
paths. Full stochastic retraining and complete figure rebuilding were not rerun
during this reorganization. Some original scripts require source-layout or
software-path adaptation, as identified in the archive README. Do not treat
hash checks or fixed-model replay as an end-to-end refit validation.

S5 supplies a restoration/checking entry point for consolidated final docking
files. Coordinate-based RMSD, seed-score and contact checks were repeated; no new
Vina searches were performed. Raw-structure preprocessing and rendering require
their recorded external software. The prepared-input workflow and its tested
scope are distinguished from cold-start structural preparation.

## Source access and rights

Public transcriptome accessions are GSE66360, GSE48060, GSE61144 and GSE59867.
The supplied expression matrices are processed values, not raw CEL files or
participant PFOS measurements. Original raw data remain accessible through GEO.
Provider-restricted database exports are not newly redistributed; standardized
scores and source provenance are supplied. Consult RIGHTS_AND_SOURCES.md and
DATA_DICTIONARY.md before reuse.
